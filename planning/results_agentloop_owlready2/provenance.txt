The build: facts asserted on Owlready2's objects (no inference); every step the previous inferences are deleted
(ontology.destroy()) and Pellet is run again with sync_reasoner_pellet(infer_property_values=True), which exports the
world and writes the inferences back into an ontology of their own; candidates by world.search; the planner runs in
Python on the answers, once per distinct room.

1,358,555 inferences are deleted and 1,358,558 statements inserted (the step's asserted facts and the inferences
